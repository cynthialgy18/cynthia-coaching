Questions

Frequently Asked

Is life coaching the same as therapy? +

No — they're quite different. Therapy typically focuses on healing past wounds and diagnosing mental health conditions. Coaching focuses on your present situation and future goals. We work forward, not backward. If you need therapeutic support, I'll always refer you to the right resource.

How long does it take to see results? +

Many clients notice meaningful shifts in clarity and confidence within the first 2–3 sessions. Deeper, lasting change typically unfolds over 3–6 months. The timeline depends on what you're working on and how actively you engage between sessions.

Do sessions happen in person or online? +

All sessions are conducted online via video call (Zoom or your preferred platform). This means we can work together no matter where in the world you are — and you can join from the comfort of your own home.

What if coaching isn't right for me? +

That's exactly what the free discovery call is for. If I don't think I can genuinely help you, I'll tell you honestly — and point you toward something that can. I'd rather you find the right support than the wrong fit.
