My Story

The Coach Behind the Journey

I didn't start out as a life coach — I started out just like you. Here's how a personal crisis became my greatest gift.

My Background

From Burned Out to Breaking Free

For over a decade I did everything "right." Prestigious job, steady promotions, a packed schedule that left no room to breathe. On the outside, I was thriving. On the inside, I was running on empty — and I had no idea why.

The turning point came during a routine meeting. I realised I couldn't remember the last time I felt genuinely excited about my work — or my life. That quiet, terrifying moment of clarity changed everything.

I spent the next two years doing the hard inner work: therapy, coaching, reading, retreating. I discovered the frameworks and practices that actually move the needle. And I found my calling: helping others navigate the same crossroads with less confusion and more courage.

Today, I'm a certified life coach with 7+ years of experience helping mid-career adults — people in their 30s, 40s, and 50s — rediscover what matters, shed what doesn't, and build lives they're proud of.
